# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a respectful, harassment-free collaboration space regardless of background, identity,
experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, constructive, and patient.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Respect privacy and operational-security boundaries.
- Accept responsibility, correct mistakes, and prioritize community safety.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disclosure of private information, and
sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Maintainers may
edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or ban participants when necessary. Reports are
handled as confidentially as practical, with attention to the safety of affected people.
